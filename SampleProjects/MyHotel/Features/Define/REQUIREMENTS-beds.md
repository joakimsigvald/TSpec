# Beds per room

A room has from one to nine beds.

- Adding or updating a room with fewer than one or more than nine beds is refused.

In the UI:

- "Sängar" is a dropdown of 1 to 9, in the add-room form and on each room's row, so no other number
  can be chosen.
- In the add-room form it shows 1 until another number is chosen, and 1 again after adding.
